## Description
Hub assembly with a chain sprocket and mounting flange.

## Why it was made
Adapting a chain drive to a new shaft.

## What it solved
Sprocket can be swapped without removing the shaft.

## Challenges
Ensuring concentricity between hub and sprocket.

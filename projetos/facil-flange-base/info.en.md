## Description
Turned flange with a central recess and a hex nut welded to the tab.

## Why it was made
Modeling exercise for turned parts using revolve features.

## What it solved
Practice in dimensioning and fit tolerances.

## Challenges
Placing the nut while respecting the tab thickness.

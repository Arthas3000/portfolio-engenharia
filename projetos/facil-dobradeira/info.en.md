## Description
Bench device for bending thin sheet metal, with a clamping bar and side levers.

## Why it was made
The shop relied on third parties for simple bends, delaying small jobs.

## What it solved
Bends up to 2 mm are now done in-house, cutting lead time.

## Challenges
Keeping the clamping bar rigid without making the assembly too heavy.
